Who she is

Nurse Gaba and Tracey worked together in nursing before either of them picked up a cosmetic needle. Gaba went on to train in medical aesthetics, and now treats clients here at Bonnie Lass Beauty alongside Nurse Practitioner Tracey Croft, with the same calm, careful approach in the treatment chair.

Her background

A Bachelor of Nursing and a Post Graduate qualification in Neonatal Intensive Care, followed by training in cosmetic nursing. Looking after the smallest and most fragile patients is a discipline of precision and patience, and it shows in how she works: structured, methodical, and unwilling to cut a corner. Ethics and safety come first with Gaba, every time, and clients notice.

Booking with Gaba

Gaba is in clinic most Mondays. When booking you can select your practitioner, and her availability will show in the calendar so you can pick a time that suits you. Every treatment still starts with a consultation, and the same assessment-first approach applies whoever you see.

What stays the same

  • A consultation before any treatment
  • Honest advice, including when the answer is no
  • Natural results, never an overdone look
