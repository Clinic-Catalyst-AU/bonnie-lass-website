Start here

Three rules that keep it looking like this

You do not need to be a designer. If a new page uses these colours, these two typefaces and these blocks, it will sit beside the rest of the site without anyone noticing it was added later.

  • Navy, gold and ivory only. No new colours. Gold is for small accents (eyebrow lines, prices, one button), never for big areas.
  • Cormorant Garamond for headings, Montserrat for everything else. Headings are light, large and never bold. One italic word in a heading is the house style.
  • Concern first, treatment second. Every page opens with what she sees in the mirror, in her words. Compliance notes live at the bottom of every page, not hidden.
The July brand guide listed a warm gold on near-black with teal and Open Sans. The site as approved and built uses navy, gold and ivory with Montserrat. This document is the source of truth for the website. The July guide still applies to voice.

Imagery

Calm clinic, real people, never results

All photography is from the Daydream shoots (January, June and August 2026). Natural light, the clinic's own florals and packs, Tracey and the team in uniform. Square corners, no filters, no stock.

  • Hero images: 4:5 or 16:9 crops, object-position centre top so faces never crop.
  • Card images: 3:2. Treatment side images: 4:5. Always add an alt line that names the clinic and Melton.
  • Export JPEG at 1400px wide, quality 80 to 85. Logo PNGs: gold on light, white on navy, black for print.
  • Before and after photos go in the gated gallery only, with written consent on file. Never on a public page, never in an ad.
  • Do not publish the lip-feature wall poster (BMT_0595). It advertises a prescription-only outcome.

Voice

A nurse who gets it, not a salesperson

Calm, warm and honest. Speaks to the woman first, the skin second. "You" far more than "we". Short, unhurried sentences. The consult is a conversation, not a commitment.

AHPRA and TGA non-negotiables

  • Concern-led, never claim-led. Never promise a treatment outcome or a timeline.
  • Never name or price a Schedule 4 treatment in public copy. Injectable conversations happen in the room.
  • No testimonials about regulated treatments. No before and after as a promise.
  • "Has a special interest in", never "specialist" or "expert". Every treatment page carries the compliance note.
  • Prices are shown where they are fixed. Where a treatment is prescribed, say "consultation required" rather than "from".

Building new pages in GHL so they match

The site runs on one stylesheet. Load it once at site level in GHL and every new page inherits the tokens, buttons and blocks above.

1. Site-level header code

Sites > Settings > Head tracking code. Paste the font link and the stylesheet link, then the tokens block so GHL elements can use the variables too.

4. Tracking already wired

Meta pixel, Meta domain verification, Google Tag Manager GTM-5XDVCDC and GA4 are documented in the site build. Do not add a second pixel or GA tag inside a page. Everything goes at site level.
