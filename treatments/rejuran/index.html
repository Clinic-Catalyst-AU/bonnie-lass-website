Rejuran

Repairs the skin from the inside rather than filling it from the outside.

Duration30 minutes From$395 Performed byOne of our nurses

What it is

Rejuran uses polynucleotides to work at a cellular level - stimulating collagen, improving elasticity and strengthening the skin barrier itself.

Who it suits

Thin, crepey, tired skin. Around the eyes it thickens and revitalises delicate skin without adding volume, which is why it suits people who do not want to look filled.

What a course looks like

Discussed at consultation.

What you can expect

  • Improved texture and hydration
  • Firmer, stronger skin barrier
  • Reduced fine lines and pores
  • Natural result, no added volume

A consultation with a nurse practitioner is required before this treatment to assess your suitability. Individual results vary and are not guaranteed. Any procedure carries risks, which will be discussed with you.
