Pricing FAQ

Why do some treatments have a price and others do not?

Because some are purchased and some are prescribed. Skin treatments like PRX-Plus, BYRYZN, Rejuran and microneedling have a fixed price, and it is published on this page. Injectable treatments depend on your face, the area and the amount required - quoting a number before Tracey has assessed you would be a guess, so we do not.

Will I get a price before anything happens?

Yes. You will know exactly what a treatment costs before you agree to it. Nobody is billed for a surprise.

Are packages cheaper?

Where a treatment genuinely needs a course, yes. The microneedling course of four with NCTF is $1,170, which saves $130. The exosome course of four is $1,350, saving $150. The packages exist because the course is what produces the result, not as a sales tactic.

Do you offer gift vouchers?

Yes. Ask in clinic or call 0434 710 010.

What is the cheapest way to start?

The free skin assessment, then the consultation. Both cost nothing, and Tracey will frequently tell you that the highest-value thing you can do is skincare and sun protection - which is the cheapest intervention there is.
