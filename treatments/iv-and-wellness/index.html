IV Therapy and Wellness

At Bonnie Lass Beauty, your wellness goals come first.

Every IV therapy is guided by our nurse-led clinical team, delivered with careful clinical oversight and genuine care for your wellbeing, because how we look after you matters just as much as the treatment itself.

IV Vitamin Drips

Each IV vitamin drip is formulated from a tailored selection of vitamins, minerals and amino acids, delivered directly into the bloodstream and paired with intravenous hydration. Every formulation is administered by our nurse-led clinical team as part of a personalised approach to complementing your nutrient intake and overall wellness routine.

Our clinical formulations draw from a comprehensive range of vitamins and amino acids, including: Vitamin C, B Complex (B1-B5), B Complex Plus (B1-B6 + B12), Zinc, Selenium, Magnesium, GABA, Taurine, Serine, Carnitine, Inositol, Methionine, Choline, Methylcobalamin (B12), Glycine, Arginine, Lysine, Ornithine, Glutamine, Biotin and Glutathione.

Intramuscular and Subcutaneous Shots

Our intramuscular and subcutaneous shots deliver a concentrated, single vitamin or mineral in a quick, fifteen-minute dose, perfect for adding to your IV drip as part of a tailored treatment plan.

Blood Testing

Our in-clinic micronutrient blood testing, conducted in partnership with iScreen, provides a biomarker profile of your current vitamin and mineral status. This clinical insight allows our team to guide a wellness plan tailored specifically to your individual needs. Available in-clinic only; enquire with our team during your consultation.

How Booking Works

Every IV therapy, shot and blood panel begins with a consultation with our clinical team, ensuring your wellness plan is considered and appropriately tailored before any treatment is administered. Following your consultation, our team will arrange the appropriate drip, shot or test for your visit.

A consultation with a registered nurse is required before this treatment to assess your suitability. Individual results vary and are not guaranteed. Any procedure carries risks, which will be discussed with you.
