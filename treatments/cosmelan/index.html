Pigmentation

Cosmelan Pigmentation Treatment

A professional-grade pigmentation treatment, delivered in two phases.

PhasesIn clinic, then at home DowntimeVisible peeling Delivered byOur nurse-led clinical team

Cosmelan is a professional-grade pigmentation treatment, delivered in two phases by our nurse-led clinical team: an intensive in-clinic application followed by a structured at-home care phase. It is designed for those looking to target stubborn pigmentation, including sun-induced pigmentation, melasma and uneven skin tone.

Understanding Pigmentation

Pigmentation concerns like sun-induced spots, melasma and post-inflammatory marks can be difficult to shift with skincare alone. Cosmelan is formulated to target the visible appearance of pigmentation at a deeper level, working alongside a structured take-home routine to help manage how it presents over time.

How the Treatment Works

Cosmelan is applied in two phases. The first is an in-clinic application performed by our clinical team, followed by a take-home phase using Cosmelan's complementary formulation to support your skin over the following weeks. Because this is an intensive treatment, visible peeling is a normal part of the process, and your treatment plan will be tailored to your skin and pigmentation concerns at your consultation.

What to Expect

Every Cosmelan treatment begins with a consultation so we can assess your skin and discuss what is involved, including the expected downtime and home care commitment. Results vary from person to person, and our team will guide you through what is realistic for your skin.

Real Results

Following your consultation, you will be given a gallery of real client results, shared with permission from those who have chosen to show their journey.

A consultation with a registered nurse is required before this treatment to assess your suitability. Individual results vary and are not guaranteed. Any procedure carries risks, which will be discussed with you.
